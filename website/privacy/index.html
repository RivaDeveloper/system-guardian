Privacy

Guardian runs on your PC. This site has no accounts, no cookies and no trackers.

What the app sends

  • Update check: it asks GitHub for the newest Guardian release, and downloads definitions (known-bad hash lists and signature rules) from their public sources.
  • VirusTotal (optional, off by default): only a file's SHA-256 fingerprint is sent, never the file, and only with your own VirusTotal key.

What it never does

  • It never uploads your files and never opens your documents, photos or ID scans.
  • It never removes anything on its own that you can't undo: quarantine is a move you can reverse.

Your settings, history and quarantine stay on your PC in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Guardian.
